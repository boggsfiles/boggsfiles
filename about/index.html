About
the Files

Hi! Boggsfiles is an X-Files archive by fans and philes, for anyone who loves the show as much as we do. It started as one collection and got a little out of hand.

What's in here

Everything in the files

Scripts in all their color-coded revisions, raw dailies, wrap-party gag reels, call sheets, shooting schedules, cast lists and a lot of paper nobody was supposed to keep. Plus every episode transcribed, screencapped and mapped to where it was actually filmed.

Where it comes from

Fans, mostly

A lot of this came from fans and collectors who trusted the archive with it. Some want credit, some want to stay anonymous, and both are respected here. It's all here for fans, writers, researchers and anyone who's ever paused an episode to read the paper on Mulder's desk. Come say hi on Instagram at @boggsfiles 👽
