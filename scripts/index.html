Why the colors?

During production, revised script pages were issued in different colors so the cast and crew could quickly identify newer changes.

WhiteBluePinkYellowGreenGoldenrodSalmon
Not every episode has every stage. Later revisions may repeat the sequence as Second Blue, Second Pink, and so on.
