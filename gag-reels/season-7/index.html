A gag reel is the blooper tape the crew cuts together for the wrap party at the end of a season. Flubbed lines, broken takes, corpsing, and the running jokes that only make sense if you were on that set. These are the full reels, taken straight off the discs rather than a tape dub of a tape dub.

New: an alternative Season 7 gag reel, a second, unofficial cut. It runs 17:29 against the DVD reel’s 11:37. Almost all of it is different: only a handful of moments, the crew photo outside Stage 8 among them, are in both. The picture is cropped to widescreen. A few shots are marked NO AUDIO and one stretch is sound only, under a WILD TRACK card. That is how the reel was cut, so nothing is wrong with the player.

Season 7 of The X-Files aired from November 7, 1999 to May 21, 2000, shot in Los Angeles.
