A gag reel is the blooper tape the crew cuts together for the wrap party at the end of a season. Flubbed lines, broken takes, corpsing, and the running jokes that only make sense if you were on that set. These are the full reels, taken straight off the discs rather than a tape dub of a tape dub.

Season 8 of The X-Files aired from November 5, 2000 to May 20, 2001, shot in Los Angeles.
