The Field Where
I Died Sides

Gillian Anderson’s sides from The Field Where I Died: script pages, a call sheet, and Scully’s lines written out by hand on the blank backs, again and again, with doodles in between.

17 pages4X05September 1996

The typed page reads “Because hypnosis is used in treatment of Dissociative Identity.” The pen adds “the,” every handwritten copy says “the treatment,” and that is how the line aired. From a private collection. Click a page to see it whole.
