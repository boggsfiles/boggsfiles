Terma Sides

Gillian Anderson’s sides from Terma: the 2nd unit call sheet, four script pages, and Scully’s nursing home lines written out by hand on the blank backs.

7 pages4X10November 1996

The typed page reads “Who gave the patients their meds tonight?” Every handwritten copy says “these patients,” and that is how the line aired. The call sheet on top is the 2nd unit’s for November 6, 1996 at Woodlands in New Westminster, with Paper Hearts inserts on the same sheet. From a private collection. Click a page to see it whole.
