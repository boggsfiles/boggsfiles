2nd Unit &
Production Schedules

Thirty-one documents from inside the production office: the memos that told every department where the second unit would be on Thursday, the schedules that mapped whole seasons before the episodes had names, and the calendars that counted a month down to the minute.

31 documents181 scanned pagesSeasons 5, 7, 8 and 91997–2002

Second unit is the part of a production almost nobody keeps paperwork from. It shoots what the main unit cannot: inserts, pickups, hands and feet, plates, reshoots of a scene that did not cut together. Somebody has to tell every department where it will be and what it needs, so an assistant director writes a memo, runs it off on whatever color the revision has reached, and distributes it. Then it is superseded, usually within days, and thrown away. These survived. Each one is scanned in full color on its original stock, because the color is the revision.

Insert lists

2 documents

Inserts are the close ups the main unit still owes: the hands, the objects, the things an edit cuts to. Somebody has to keep track of which ones are outstanding. Two of those lists survived, and one of them is handwritten.

Tuesday, November 4, 1997  ·  5X05  ·  1 page

Christmas Carol, pickups and inserts

Page two of the pickup and insert list, on goldenrod. Almost every line is a point of view: Scully comparing photographs, a hypodermic needle with blood in a baggy, two PCR sheets held to the light, the crucifix in the jewellery box. The header carries a typo that nobody caught, CHISTMAS CAROL, and the shoot day line reads November 1999 on a sheet dated November 1997.

Undated  ·  5X06  ·  1 page

The Post-Modern Prometheus, preliminary inserts

The script supervisor's own list, written out by hand in pen on a blank form. Inserts are the close ups the main unit still owes, and this is somebody tracking what is outstanding as the day goes. Scene 60, in the cellar, reads as a shopping list: CS SANDWICH ON PLATE, CS GOAT, CS HORSE, CS ROOSTER, CS PIG, CS WINDOWS GET SMASHED. Scene 1 is CU GOAT BOY LKS INTO CAR. The circled M and S mark whose eyeline each shot has to match, so Mulder and Scully are being tracked shot by shot. The last line on the page is TV PLAYING JERRY SPRINGER.

Pink  ·  Revised August 20, 1997  ·  1 page

Fifth Season Directors Schedule

The whole season on one page: show number, writer, director, prep, start and wrap. Episodes 1 and 2 are typed. Episodes 3 and 4 have their titles written in by hand in blue pen, Redux II and Detour, because they had not been named when this was typed. From episode 5 on, the title column is simply empty: directors and dates assigned to episodes that did not exist yet. The sheet heads itself DIRECTORS SCHEDULE, with no apostrophe, and the title here follows the paper.

Je Souhaite, Season 7

2 documents

Two documents from one episode, 7ABX21, written and directed by Vince Gilligan and shot through April 2000. They arrived bound into a single file and are separated here, because they were kept by two different people. The first belonged to the art department. The second belonged to somebody in effects, and it is the more interesting of the two.

April 6, 2000  ·  from Corey Kaplan and Lauren Polizzi  ·  7ABX21  ·  16 pages

Je Souhaite, director's plans

An art department memo and the fourteen set drawings that answer it, for production designer Corey Kaplan, drawn by J. Bruce, Harbour and RH/JB. The memo is headed “Untitled”: four days before the first day of shooting, Je Souhaite still had no name. The sets run from the Avalon Carson trailer park and the mobile home interior on Stage 5 to the U-Stor-It on Pacific Coast Highway, the Elysee Cafe standing in for the diner, 5th and Spring downtown, the county morgue, Mulder's apartment, Skinner's office and Mulder's office. Each sheet carries its own scale, and the mobile home drawing is annotated BOAT NOT ON STAGE. KICKER WALLS TO BE USED.

April 2000  ·  7ABX21  ·  12 pages

Je Souhaite, effects crew working file

Bound into the back of the same file, and not an art department document at all. Two pre-production calendars on green and pink stock, the April 6 tech scout itinerary, a yellow revised schedule, a pink production meeting pass, a handwritten crew call and the green final shooting schedule. What ties them together is the handwriting, and the handwriting belongs to somebody in effects. Beside each stop on the scout is what that stop needs: 2 cobweb guns, dust gun, water truck at the storage facility, water splash, car hood, car body on Suburban Road, and a flat NOTHING beside the diner and the city street. The handwritten page is a truck load-out: cob webbers, air hoses, a dust gun, a bucket of Fuller's earth, an air compressor, a mole fogger. The schedule margins keep the day's gags in a running list: trailer explodes, car window blow, debris fall, rug falls, and later stove knob breaks, rear panel removes, fire bar, helium heat wave. The same crew is assigned day by day across every page: Rick, Lee, Eric, Jeff, Jeff, Ron, Randy. Beside one circled scene somebody has written DID WE GET THAT?!! Four phone numbers on the tech scout page are blurred in this scan.

Director's plans, Seasons 8 and 9

3 documents

Before an episode shoots, the art department sends every department a memo listing the sets and a drawing of each one: a floor plan with the walls, the doors and the furniture, and the stage or the street it will be built on. Three packets from the Los Angeles years, one of them with the storyboards and the second unit schedule still bound in.

January 2, 2002  ·  from Corey Kaplan and Phil Dagort  ·  9ABX12  ·  45 pages

Scary Monsters, director's plans, storyboards and second unit schedule

The fattest packet in the archive, and three documents in one binder. The memo is headed “Untitled”, two weeks before shooting, and lists nine sets. Sixteen drawings follow: the Conlon house as a site plan and an overview, its downstairs, Tommy's bedroom, the Quantico autopsy bay and Scully's office, Scully's apartment, the sheriff's office, and two sheets for a set the memo calls Limbo: a 20 by 20 fall pad at ground level for the start of the fall, and the descender. Drawn by Robert Harbour and John P. Bruce. Then twenty-one pages of storyboards for scenes 11, 17 and 25, drawn by D. Cline with each shot described in longhand beside its frame (Close on Doggett: “What the hell?”), this copy marked for a crew member in red. The last six pages are the second unit shoot schedule of January 15, 2002: three days on Stages 5 and 6, call at 3 pm, wrap at midnight, with crew assignments and FIRE, PROCESS CAR and BLOODY ENGINE written in the margin against the scenes that needed them.

Show drawings, Seasons 5 and 8

3 documents

What Vancouver called a director's plans packet, plus a set of storyboards. These sat in a folder nothing linked to until an archive-wide scan for drawing pages found them on October 17, 2026.

October 16, 1997  ·  from the Art Dept  ·  5X06  ·  13 pages

The Post-Modern Prometheus, revised show drawings

The Vancouver version of a director's plans packet, headed 5X06 “MUTATO” and stamped REVISED, with the Midnight Inquisitor on the cover and the warning that everything is subject to change. The art department's locations memo puts the farm house and barn at 2981 41B Street in Delta with the cellar built on Stage 1, the Berkowitz house at 2659 Oxford Street, J.J.'s Diner at Pat's Galley in Steveston, the Pallidori house at Century House in Deas Island Park with the bedroom on Stage 1, and the woods swale at Maplewood Flats. Eleven drawings follow: the farm site plan, the old man's barn and house, the Berkowitz house, the diner, the Pallidori house and bedroom, a hand-drawn map of the Dollarton Highway, Pallidori's lab and office. Production designer Graeme Murray, from the Brooksbank Avenue office in North Vancouver.
