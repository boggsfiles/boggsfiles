The Field Where I Died
Wardrobe File

The wardrobe department's binder for The Field Where I Died: continuity polaroids of Mulder, Scully, Melissa, Vernon Ephesian and the Temple of the Seven Stars, the costume breakdown character by character, the Blue script and shooting schedule, the Pink one-liner, the call sheets for all eight days, the location maps and the memos that went around the set in September 1996.

13 documents207 scanned pagesSeason 4 · 4X051996

A wardrobe department keeps a binder for every episode: polaroids of each costume as it was shot, so a jacket buttoned in the wide shot is buttoned in the close-up two days later, and the paperwork that tells the department who is on set when. This one survived intact. The scans were shared by a private collector, who holds the originals. Phone numbers of individuals have been blacked out; the production office line on the letterhead was already public.
