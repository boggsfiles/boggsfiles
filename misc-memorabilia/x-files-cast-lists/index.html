Cast Lists

Who played whom, episode by episode: the production office's cast lists, faxed to every department as casting came together, with each actor's agent beside the name. Seventeen typed lists from Seasons 1 to 5, and the handwritten background sheet for a day of Emily.

18 documents34 scanned pagesSeasons 1 to 51994–1998

Mulder and Scully are always first, care of the production office. Below them is everyone else the episode needed that week, from guest stars to the extras at a funeral. Click a list to read it; the arrows turn the page. Phone numbers, home addresses and the hotels where actors stayed have been blacked out, along with the union numbers on the handwritten sheet. Shapes and Humbug come from the wardrobe files shared by @scullywear.
