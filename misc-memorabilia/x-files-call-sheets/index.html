X-Files Call Sheets

Original documents, interviews, and artifacts from the Boggsfiles collection.

Original archive materialPreserved by Boggsfiles

A TV call sheet is a document that provides important information for the cast and crew of a TV show or movie's shooting day. It's usually created by the assistant director (1st AD) or someone in the AD department, and sent to the cast and crew the night before each shoot. Pay close attention to some of them as they have maps in the call sheet of where they filmed for that specific episode!
