What is a Shooting Schedule? A shooting schedule for a TV show is a document that outlines when and where each scene will be filmed, who will be involved, and what resources will be needed. It's a project management tool that helps production teams manage their time, budget, crew, cast, locations, equipment, and logistics effectively and efficiently.
