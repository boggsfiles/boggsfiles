X-Files
Show Drawings

The X-Files art department's show drawings: the floor plans, site plans and sketches handed to every department so the crew knew what was being built and where. Five sets from Seasons 4 and 5, and storyboards from Seasons 7 and 8.

7 sets75 scanned pagesSeasons 4 to 81996–2000

Most of the sets open with a cover, a few of them with a cartoon on it, and every drawing carries the show's title block: the set, the location, the production designer, the date and a drawing number. Click a page to read it; the arrows turn the page. A name on the Post-Modern Prometheus cover and two handwritten notes have been blacked out.

Unruhe

Art department, July 1996, revised. Show drawings no. 2, on blue paper, with a Life in Hell strip on the cover and a REVISED stamp. Inside are hand-drafted plans for the tomography room, the photo lab, the hospital hallway with its nurses' station, the booking room and the Dark Place. From a private collection.

4X08 · Show drawings  ·  18 pages

Paper Hearts

Art department, September and October 1996. Show drawings no. 8, with another Life in Hell strip on the cover: “Mistakes were made.” Inside are the art department's locations memo of October 4, 1996, the stage plans, the Vineyard Motel room, the airplane cabin with the hero seats marked, the Greenwich basement, the prison visiting room, hall and gym, the autopsy room, the daycare, and a hand-drawn map of the park where the forensic dig happens. Mulder's office, his apartment and Skinner's office from the Season 3 drawings are bound in too, and the last page is a sheet of handwritten notes for the shoot. From a private collection.

5X06 · Show drawings  ·  13 pages

The Post-Modern Prometheus

Art department, October 1997, revised. Filed under the working title “Mutato”, behind a cover with a Midnight Inquisitor front page of its own: “Shocked by revelations that he is God!” Inside are the art department's locations memo of October 16, 1997, and floor plans for the old man's house and barn, the Berkowitz house, J.J.'s Diner, the Pallidori house, and Pallidori's lab and office, plus a hand-drawn map of the woods.

All 13 pages
5X07 · Show drawings  ·  15 pages

Emily

Art department, October 1997. Show drawings no. 7, with a cartoon on the cover: “Aunt Vivien's Prison for Ne'er Do Wells”, and the line “On X-Files we don't bake cookies.” Inside, the Stage 2 plan with Mulder's office, the Lone Gunmen's set, the hospital MRI room and the quarantine ward, covered in handwritten notes for paint, brick and doors. Several were scanned sideways and are shown turned upright here.

All 15 pages
7X21 · Storyboards  ·  6 pages

Je Souhaite

Scenes 14, 15 and 17, April 3, 2000, pencil. Six pages for Anson's invisibility: his hand fading out in the trailer, the garbage cans, the crosswalk and the oncoming truck, ending on “BAM!!!” and “End of Act One.” One note says Jenn “bears a striking resemblance to Janeane Garofalo.”

Via Negativa

Scenes 27 to 29, pencil. Storyboards for the jail cell: Bormanis wakes to scratching, the third eye, and the rats pouring in through the bars. Two frames to a page, each with its camera direction written underneath.
