Shapes
Wardrobe File

The wardrobe department's file on Shapes: continuity polaroids of Mulder and Scully from the Montana shoot, the costume breakdown, the day breakdown, the White cast list, the scene breakdown, and the daily call sheets for seven of the eight days, the earliest call sheets on the site.

7 documents120 scanned pagesSeason 1 · 1X191994

A wardrobe department keeps a binder for every episode: polaroids of each costume as it was shot, so a jacket buttoned in the wide shot is buttoned in the close-up two days later, and the paperwork that tells the department who is on set when. This one survived intact. The scans were shared by @scullywear, who holds the originals. Phone numbers of individuals have been blacked out; the production office line on the letterhead was already public.
