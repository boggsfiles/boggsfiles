The case in brief

Case summary

Two archived drafts, the White First Draft of August 11, 1993 and the Pink Final of August 19, compared with the Region 1 DVD closed captions.

The episode reached broadcast close to its final draft. The interrogation, Ranger Brouillet's testimony and Dr. Diamond's lecture on the Wild Man myth all aired nearly word for word, trimmed for pace. One cut and two additions are the report.

Searching the vacant building, Dr. Diamond finds a blood spotted rag and, on the page, gives a biological reason for the creature's aggression: she may be in estrous. The idea is on the first draft, worded as cycling, tightened to estrous at the Blue, and carried into the Pink unchanged. The disc has “She could be injured” instead, and estrous, in heat and cycling appear nowhere on the caption track.

Two things were checked and are not claimed. A morgue exchange in which Detective Tomson asks the coroner to keep the case quiet has no caption at all. And the disc spells the detective's name TOMSON, matching the script, so there is no name change to report.

Every aired line below carries its timecode, so any of it can be checked against the disc.

Dr. Diamond's clinical read on the bloody rag, cut entirely

Pink p.38 / White p.39 · scene 37 · present in both drafts

Searching the vacant building, Dr. Diamond finds a blood spotted rag and, on the page, offers a specific biological theory for the creature's violence: she may be in estrous, which would explain the aggression. On air the beat runs straight from it being blood to her possibly being injured, with no room for the clinical line.

Revision trail: White → Blue → Pink → Aired. The idea is already in the White draft, worded there as cycling. The page carries a Blue stage stamp and an (X) mark showing the wording was tightened to estrous at that revision, and it then carries into the Pink completely unchanged. This was cut at the broadcast edit, never rewritten out of the script.

SCRIPT

DR. DIAMOND: “If it’s a female it could mean she’s in estrous. It could explain the aggressive behavior.”

AIRED · DVD CAPTIONS

“It’s blood. She could be bringing her killing here. She could be injured.” (30:40)

Estrous, in heat and cycling appear nowhere on the caption track. The track does not name a speaker at this point; the script gives the line to Dr. Diamond.

Trims through the interrogation and lab scenes

Pink pp.7–35 · not itemized

Nothing structural. Detective Tomson's standoff with Mulder, Ranger Brouillet's testimony and Dr. Diamond's lecture on the Wild Man myth all survive to air nearly word for word, tightened only for pace. There is no second cut in this episode worth naming.

Changed for screen

Two additions, both with timecodes: a tactical team nine minutes early, and three lines for Rob the page did not give him.

The tactical team arrives nine minutes early

Pink pp.38–40, scenes 37–39, against p.45, scene 50

In the script a S.W.A.T. team does not appear until the Act Four exterior cordon, where squad cars and a S.W.A.T. van form a net. The aired cut moves them up: during the earlier building search a team member is already staged outside, calling in on the radio.

AIRED ONLY · DVD CAPTIONS

“Roger. Zebra team in position.” (30:49)

That is nine minutes before the script puts them anywhere near the building.

Rob gets a warmer entrance, and a whisper is added

Pink p.20 · scene 19A · confirmed at 13:35–13:41

On the page Rob says hello and crosses to his son. The broadcast gives him a paternal beat first, three lines that exist in neither draft, and then adds a whispered aside from Ellen.

SCRIPT

ROB greets the room and crosses to his son.

AIRED · DVD CAPTIONS

“Hey, Tiger.” / “How you doing?” / “Having a good time?” (13:35–13:39) Ellen, whispered: “Divorced.” (13:41)

The script's next beat has Scully catch Rob staring and mock scold Ellen about it. Whether that played as written, a caption track cannot say.

♥ Shippy moments

Five episodes in, the blind date subplot gives Mulder something to be territorial about. All three aired as written.

“Can you cancel?”

Pink scene 25 · present in script and air · 23:25

Scully mentions she has a date, a real one this time rather than a birthday party, and Mulder's first move is to try to talk her out of it. As written, both lines.

SCRIPT

MULDER: “Can you cancel?”

AIRED · DVD CAPTIONS

“Can you cancel?” (23:25) “Unlike you, Mulder, I would like to have a life.” (23:28)

The debrief that is not a debrief

Pink scene 62 · present in script and air · 42:53–43:02

Back at the Bureau, Mulder corners Scully about Rob with the persistence of a man conducting an interview. The captions carry the whole exchange across twelve seconds.

AIRED · DVD CAPTIONS

“Same guy you had dinner with the other night?” (42:53) “You going to have dinner with him again?” (42:57) “No interest.” (43:01) / “Not at this time.” (43:02)

The page has Scully avoiding his eyes through this, then going to the Smithsonian with Mulder instead.

“Eight million years out of Africa.”

Pink scenes 39 and 62 · present in script and air · 31:47 and 43:14

Mulder's line about how little separates us from the beast woman bookends the episode: he says it at 31:47 and again at 43:14, and two seconds later she opens a door for him.

AIRED · DVD CAPTIONS

“Eight million years out of Africa…” (43:14) “And look who’s holding the door.” (43:16)

As written.
