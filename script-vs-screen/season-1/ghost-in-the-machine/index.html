The case in brief

Case summary

Two archived drafts, the White first draft of September 1, 1993 and the 2nd Blue of September 21, compared line by line with the Region 1 DVD closed captions. Seven revision stages sit between them (Blue, Pink, Green, Yellow, Goldenrod, Salmon and a second White) and none is held as a separate document, so some of the changes below can be dated to a fortnight but not to a day.

The big change is on the page, not the disc. The White ends at Jerry Lamana's funeral in Bethesda, with Mulder telling Jerry's son "We were partners," the words Jerry corrected him with in Act One. The 2nd Blue ends on the Central Operating System's camera coming back to life, and the disc adds a line even the 2nd Blue does not have: Peterson's "I'm going to figure this thing out... if it kills me" (44:31). Also aired only: the machine's "Welcome back, Brad" (21:05), and Mulder's "Hey, Jerry. It's good to see you" shortened to "Jerry?" (04:01). Cut in the edit: Scully's answering machine scene at 1:35 in the morning, her outgoing message and Agent Spiller's field report deadline, which the captions show as seventeen seconds of nothing between the phone ringing (30:41) and "Oh, my God..." (31:05).

Stage directions, who reaches for whom at the reunion, what Mulder does while Scully is in the air duct, are reported from the page only; the caption track cannot confirm or deny what the picture shows. The final draft PDF in the archive is labeled 1x07, which is Ice's number; it is the 2nd Blue of this episode.

3 findings

Cut from screen

The funeral ending, gone by the shooting draft, and Scully's answering machine scene, gone in the edit.

“We were partners...”: the White draft's funeral ending

White pp.58–59 / 2nd Blue p.55 · scene 69 · White draft only

In the White draft of September 1 the episode does not end on the Central Operating System's red light. It ends days later, at Jerry Lamana's funeral at a house in Bethesda, Maryland. Mulder crosses the yard to an eight year old boy sitting by himself, Tony, and crouches down to talk to him.

WHITE DRAFT ONLY · THE LAST LINES OF THE EPISODE

MULDER: “I was a friend of your father's. We worked together.” (after a beat) “We were partners...” (Tony regards him with curious interest; Mulder remembers Jerry.)

The line pays off a correction from Act One. Mulder introduces Jerry to Scully as someone he “worked together” with, and Jerry corrects him: “‘Worked together?’ What are you talking about, ‘worked together.’ We were partners.” That exchange is on both drafts and on the disc. On the White, Mulder uses Jerry's word to Jerry's son, and the episode ends on it. On the disc the correction is still there and the funeral is not.

The same rewrite changed the reconciliation scene. On the White, Mulder and Jerry make up over drinks at the Six Brothers Tavern, with a bartender refilling Jerry's glass, Jerry asking for another round and Mulder telling him to hold off. The 2nd Blue moves the conversation to an FBI hallway, with no drinks.

Revision trail: White 09/01/93, the funeral. The 2nd Blue's camera ending carries a Green page stamp of September 15, so the replacement was written within two weeks of the first draft. The Blue and Pink between them are not held.

“This is Special Agent Spiller...”: Scully's answering machine, 1:35 in the morning

2nd Blue pp.49–51 · scene 45 · present in both drafts

On the page, Scully's phone rings twice at 1:35 in the morning. She answers to a burst of push tones, gets up and walks into the living room, where her own outgoing message is playing and the machine is taking a message from Agent Spiller about a field report deadline. Only then does she see that her computer has switched itself on and placed the call.

SCRIPT · BOTH DRAFTS

“You've reached 555-6431. At the tone, please leave your name, number, and the time you called...” SPILLER'S VOICE (filtered): “This is Special Agent Spiller... We need you to file your field report by --”

AIRED · 30:41–31:05

( ringing ) [17-second gap] “Oh, my God...”

The phone rings at 30:41 and the next caption is “Oh, my God...” at 31:05, with nothing captioned between them. “Leave your name” and “field report” appear nowhere on the track.

Revision trail: the scene is on the White and, unchanged, on the 2nd Blue, whose pages for it still carry Green and Salmon stamps. Cut in the edit.

Wilczek's cell and the Peterson standoff, trimmed

2nd Blue pp.44–53 and 61–65

Wilczek's speeches in the jail cell and the standoff with Peterson are shorter on the disc than on the page, line by line, with the same beats in the same order. Listed so the record shows the scenes were checked.

3 findings

Changed for screen

Three lines on no page: the last words of the episode, the machine's greeting, and Mulder's "Jerry?"

“I'm going to figure this thing out... if it kills me.”: the last line, on no page

2nd Blue p.69 · final scene

The 2nd Blue ends without dialogue: the dead machine's security camera stirs, its red light comes on, fade out. The disc adds Peterson's line.

AIRED ONLY · 44:31

“I'm going to figure this thing out... if it kills me.”

The captions put it at 44:31, after twenty four seconds with nothing captioned, the wordless sting the page describes. The White cannot be checked, because it has a different ending altogether (see Cut from Screen). The 2nd Blue's last page carries a Green stamp and has no line here, so it was added after the draft on file, on set or in pages not held.

“Welcome back, Brad.”

2nd Blue p.34 · scene 26

On the page the machine says nothing until Wilczek has worked his way in. On the disc it speaks first, and knows his name.

SCRIPT · 2ND BLUE

Wilczek types. “You're not equipped with a voice synthesizer.” But COS remains silent. “What is my user level?”

AIRED · 21:05

“Welcome back, Brad.”

Revision trail: the page carries the 2nd Blue stamp of September 21, the last pass on file, with no line for the machine. On the White the character is Steven Wilczek; by the 2nd Blue he is Brad, the name the machine uses.

“Hey, Jerry. It's good to see you.” became “Jerry?”

2nd Blue p.10 · scene 7

On both drafts Jerry makes the first move, an eager hand on Mulder's shoulder, and Mulder's reaction is written as mixed, glad to see him and not so glad. The disc keeps one word of his greeting.

SCRIPT · BOTH DRAFTS

MULDER: “Hey, Jerry. It's good to see you.”

AIRED · 04:01

“Jerry?”

Who moves toward whom is blocking, and the caption track cannot say.

3 findings

♥ Shippy moments

Not much. Three lines, all as written.

“Maybe it wouldn't be such a bad idea if you talked to someone.”

2nd Blue pp.47–48 · scene 43 · in both script and air

Scully interrupts the argument about the machine to say she is worried about him: “and I think it has something to do with Jerry” (29:38), then “Maybe it wouldn't be such a bad idea if you talked to someone” (29:42). Both as written.

“Come on, Scully.”

2nd Blue pp.57–58 · scenes 55 to 58 · in both script and air

Mulder sends her into the air duct and waits. “Come on, Scully” is at 36:14, and the next fifty seconds carry no caption at all while she is out of sight. What the page has him doing while he waits cannot be checked against the track.

“Mulder, put in the disk.”

2nd Blue p.65 · scene 61 · in both script and air

Scully has spent the episode calling Mulder's theory an alibi. At gunpoint, with Peterson telling her she will be held accountable, she backs him.

AIRED · 40:51

“Mulder, put in the disk.”

It comes straight after Peterson's “you will be held accountable.” As written.
