Born Again

Raw footage from the set, take by take.

“Born Again” is episode 22 of season 1 of The X-Files (production code 1X21), first aired April 29, 1994.

Also for Born Again:Screencaps·Transcript·Scripts

8 video files

Dailies are the raw, unedited footage recorded during a day of filming. They often include slates, repeated takes, alternate performances, and material that never appears in the finished episode. The production team reviewed them to evaluate performances, coverage, focus, sound, and continuity before the episode was edited.

Half an hour of dailies, including a wild line recorded for scene 28. Every take of a scene is gathered here into one video, in the order it appears on the tape, and the slate at the head of each take gives its setup and take number.
