Teliko

Raw footage from the set, take by take.

“Teliko” is episode 3 of season 4 of The X-Files (production code 4X04), first aired October 18, 1996.

Also for Teliko:Screencaps·Transcript·Scripts

7 video files

Dailies are the raw, unedited footage recorded during a day of filming. They often include slates, repeated takes, alternate performances, and material that never appears in the finished episode. The production team reviewed them to evaluate performances, coverage, focus, sound, and continuity before the episode was edited.

Second unit, mostly at night: the bus stop, the demolition site and the air ducts, the empty quarantine room and a few inserts. No Mulder or Scully in these. Every take of a scene is gathered here into one video, in the order it appears on the tapes, and the slate at the head of each take gives its setup and take number.
