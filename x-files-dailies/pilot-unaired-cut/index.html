The Unaired Pilot

A screener cut of the Pilot, May 7, 1993: a scene that never aired, and six that run longer than on TV.

7 video filesOriginal archive materialPreserved by Boggsfiles

A screener cut of the Pilot dated May 7, 1993, about two minutes longer than the broadcast. The first clip is its opening: Scully teaching at the FBI Academy, Quantico, over a cadaver, and the note summoning her to Section Chief Blevins, then a longer arrival at FBI Headquarters under the opening credits. The aired episode starts her at the Hoover building. The Script vs. Screen report on the Pilot lists this classroom scene as written and cut; here it is, shot. The other six are scenes that run longer in this cut than on air: the flight to Oregon, the cemetery, the walk with Dr. Glass, the rain, the diner with Theresa and the forest. Each clip starts a few seconds before the extra footage and ends a few seconds after it, so you can see where it was trimmed.
