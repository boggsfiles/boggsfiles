Dailies are the raw, unedited footage recorded during a day of filming. They often include slates, repeated takes, alternate performances, and material that never appears in the finished episode. The production team reviewed them to evaluate performances, coverage, focus, sound, and continuity before the episode was edited.

These scenes were pulled from tape parts 9, 10 and 12 and the full 4X08 reel. Takes that appear on more than one tape are kept once. Every take of a scene is gathered here into one video, in the order it appears on the tape, and the slate at the head of each take gives its setup and take number. The shorter parts follow the scenes.
