Deep Throat

Raw footage from the set, take by take.

“Deep Throat” is episode 2 of season 1 of The X-Files (production code 1X01), first aired September 17, 1993.

Also for Deep Throat:Screencaps·Transcript·Scripts

9 video files

Dailies are the raw, unedited footage recorded during a day of filming. They often include slates, repeated takes, alternate performances, and material that never appears in the finished episode. The production team reviewed them to evaluate performances, coverage, focus, sound, and continuity before the episode was edited.

Two reels from the first episode after the Pilot. The second carries tape damage, left as it is. Every take of a scene is gathered here into one video, in the order it appears on the tape, and the slate at the head of each take gives its setup and take number.
