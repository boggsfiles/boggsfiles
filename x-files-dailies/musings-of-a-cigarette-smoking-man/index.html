Musings of a Cigarette Smoking Man

Raw footage from the set, take by take.

“Musings of a Cigarette Smoking Man” is episode 7 of season 4 of The X-Files (production code 4X07), first aired November 17, 1996.

18 video files

Dailies are the raw, unedited footage recorded during a day of filming. They often include slates, repeated takes, alternate performances, and material that never appears in the finished episode. The production team reviewed them to evaluate performances, coverage, focus, sound, and continuity before the episode was edited.

Six tapes, two of which were copies of two others, so every take appears here once. Every take of a scene is gathered here into one video, in the order it appears on the tape, and the slate at the head of each take gives its setup and take number.
