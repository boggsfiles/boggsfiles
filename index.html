03: CASE FILES
5X12 · Blue pages · Jan 7, 1998

Bad Blood

Vince Gilligan wrote one line for Mulder that never made it to air: “Very good, Scully! Very good.” But it’s the stage directions around it that really get me: “Mulder grins big, delighted. He’s really proud of her.” Wish we could have seen this!!
