Not on the map

Stages. Season 1 began on rented stages at the old Molson Brewery site in Kitsilano, where the Icy Cape research station in "Ice" and Scully's apartment interior were built. Partway through the season the show moved to North Shore Studios in North Vancouver, its home for the rest of the Vancouver years. Stage 2 there is listed for every episode.

Sources disagree. Squeeze: production records put the parkade at 107 E. Cordova St; another source says a Front Street ramp in New Westminster. Fallen Angel: production records say the microwave station was B.C. Hydro's control centre on Burnaby Mountain; another source says SFU's Water Tower Building. Shapes: production records place Bordertown on 224th St in Maple Ridge; another source says Mission. The pins follow the production records.

Stock footage. Establishing shots of the FBI building, the Hotel Vancouver exterior in "Fire," and the courthouse in "Miracle Man" are stock shots, not Season 1 location work.

How to read the pins. Red pins are confirmed from production records. Green pins were identified from the episode itself and public filming records. Amber pins come from a single listing that gives an address without saying what was shot there. Where records disagree on a street number, the pin follows the production record and the note gives both. Map lines © OpenStreetMap contributors.
