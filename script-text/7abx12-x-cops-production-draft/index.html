A NOTE TO THE READER: What follows, from beginning to end, is an
episode of “The X~Files” which looks in every way, shape and
form like an hour-long episode of the television show “Cops.” It
will be shot in its entirety on Betacam 600 video cameras in
handheld, “gun-and-run” style. It will employ the “Cops” theme
music and, in some fashion, the “Cops” main title sequence.
Furthermore, you may notice a bit of cursing in the dialog
understand that all of the unacceptable words will be bleeped in
post-production. Bad boys, bad boys...
